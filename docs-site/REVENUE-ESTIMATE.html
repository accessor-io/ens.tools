Fee Structure

Fixed Fees (in ETH)

  • Contract Registration: 0.01 ETH (~$25-30)
  • ENS Name Registration: 0.005 ETH (~$12-15)
  • Subdomain Creation: 0.003 ETH (~$7-9)
  • Domain Transfer: 0.002 ETH (~$5-6)

    • Variable Fees

  • Marketplace Fee: 2.5% of sale price (250 basis points)

    • - Applied to all marketplace sales - For premium names: Full sale proceeds go to ENS DAO, you keep 2.5% fee - For regular names: Seller gets proceeds minus 2.5% fee, you get 2.5% fee

    Market Context

    Based on ENS protocol revenue data:

  • Q1 2025: $4.94M total ENS revenue
  • Q3 2024: $6.28M total ENS revenue
  • 2023: $18.9M total revenue
  • 2022: $50.2M total revenue (peak year)

    • ENS marketplace activity (OpenSea):

  • Average ENS domain price: ~0.1-1 ETH
  • Premium names: Can sell for 10-100+ ETH
  • Monthly sales volume: Varies significantly

    • Revenue Projections

    Scenario 1: Conservative (Low Adoption)

    Assumptions:
  • 10 contract registrations/month
  • 50 ENS name registrations/month
  • 100 subdomain creations/month
  • 20 domain transfers/month
  • $10,000/month marketplace volume (regular names)
  • $5,000/month premium name sales
    • Monthly Revenue:
  • Fixed fees: ~0.8 ETH (~$2,000-2,400)
  • Marketplace fees (regular): $250 (2.5% of $10k)
  • Marketplace fees (premium): $125 (2.5% of $5k)
  • Total: ~$2,375-2,775/month
  • Annual: ~$28,500-33,300

    • Scenario 2: Moderate (Medium Adoption)

    Assumptions:
  • 50 contract registrations/month
  • 200 ENS name registrations/month
  • 500 subdomain creations/month
  • 100 domain transfers/month
  • $50,000/month marketplace volume (regular names)
  • $25,000/month premium name sales
    • Monthly Revenue:
  • Fixed fees: ~3.5 ETH (~$8,750-10,500)
  • Marketplace fees (regular): $1,250 (2.5% of $50k)
  • Marketplace fees (premium): $625 (2.5% of $25k)
  • Total: ~$10,625-12,375/month
  • Annual: ~$127,500-148,500

    • Scenario 3: Optimistic (High Adoption)

    Assumptions:
  • 200 contract registrations/month
  • 1,000 ENS name registrations/month
  • 2,000 subdomain creations/month
  • 500 domain transfers/month
  • $200,000/month marketplace volume (regular names)
  • $100,000/month premium name sales
    • Monthly Revenue:
  • Fixed fees: ~14 ETH (~$35,000-42,000)
  • Marketplace fees (regular): $5,000 (2.5% of $200k)
  • Marketplace fees (premium): $2,500 (2.5% of $100k)
  • Total: ~$42,500-49,500/month
  • Annual: ~$510,000-594,000

    • Scenario 4: Premium Name Focus (High-Value Sales)

    Assumptions:
  • 20 contract registrations/month
  • 100 ENS name registrations/month
  • 200 subdomain creations/month
  • 50 domain transfers/month
  • $20,000/month marketplace volume (regular names)
  • $200,000/month premium name sales (high-value premium names)
    • Monthly Revenue:
  • Fixed fees: ~1.5 ETH (~$3,750-4,500)
  • Marketplace fees (regular): $500 (2.5% of $20k)
  • Marketplace fees (premium): $5,000 (2.5% of $200k) ⭐
  • Total: ~$9,250-10,000/month
  • Annual: ~$111,000-120,000

    • Key Revenue Drivers

    1. Marketplace Fees (Primary Driver)

  • Regular names: 2.5% of all sales
  • Premium names: 2.5% of all sales (proceeds go to ENS DAO, you keep fee)
  • Potential: Highest revenue source as volume grows
  • Example: $1M monthly volume = $25,000/month in fees

    • 2. Premium Name Sales (High-Value Opportunity)

  • Premium names can sell for 10-100+ ETH
  • Your 2.5% fee on a 50 ETH premium sale = 1.25 ETH (~$3,125)
  • Even 1-2 premium sales/month can significantly boost revenue

    • 3. Fixed Fees (Steady Income)

  • Predictable revenue from operations
  • Scales with platform adoption
  • Lower per-transaction value but consistent

    • Market Share Assumptions

    Conservative: 0.1-0.5% of ENS marketplace volume
  • Based on new platform, limited adoption
  • Focus on niche/enterprise users
    • Moderate: 1-2% of ENS marketplace volume
  • Established platform with good UX
  • Growing user base
  • Some enterprise adoption
    • Optimistic: 5-10% of ENS marketplace volume
  • Leading ENS management platform
  • Strong brand recognition
  • Enterprise partnerships

    • Revenue Breakdown by Source

    Year 1 Projections (Moderate Scenario)

    Fixed Fees: $105,000-126,000/year
  • Contract registrations: $15,000-18,000
  • Name registrations: $14,400-18,000
  • Subdomain creations: $18,000-21,600
  • Transfers: $2,400-3,000
    • Marketplace Fees: $22,500/year
  • Regular name sales: $15,000
  • Premium name sales: $7,500
    • Total Year 1: ~$127,500-148,500

    Year 2-3 Projections (Growth Scenario)

    Assuming 2-3x growth in adoption:

  • Year 2: $250,000-300,000
  • Year 3: $400,000-500,000

    • Premium Name Revenue Potential

    Premium names represent the highest-value opportunity:

    Example Premium Sales:
  • 3-letter name: 50-200 ETH
  • 4-letter name: 10-50 ETH
  • Common word: 20-100 ETH
    • Your Fee (2.5%):
  • 50 ETH sale = 1.25 ETH (~$3,125)
  • 100 ETH sale = 2.5 ETH (~$6,250)
  • 200 ETH sale = 5 ETH (~$12,500)
    • Monthly Premium Sales Scenarios:
  • 1 premium sale/month (avg 50 ETH): $3,125/month = $37,500/year
  • 3 premium sales/month (avg 50 ETH): $9,375/month = $112,500/year
  • 5 premium sales/month (avg 50 ETH): $15,625/month = $187,500/year

    • Risk Factors

    1. Market Volatility: ENS marketplace volume fluctuates
    2. Competition: Other platforms may offer lower fees
    3. Adoption: Revenue depends on user adoption
    4. Premium Name Availability: Limited supply of premium names
    5. ETH Price: Revenue in USD varies with ETH price

      6. Growth Opportunities

    6. Enterprise Partnerships: Bulk registrations, custom pricing
    7. API Access: Charge for API usage
    8. Premium Features: Subscription tiers for advanced features
    9. White-Label Solutions: License platform to other organizations
    10. Cross-Chain Expansion: Support more L2s increases addressable market

    Recommended Estimate

    Year 1 (Moderate Scenario): $100,000-150,000
  • Conservative: $30,000-50,000
  • Moderate: $100,000-150,000
  • Optimistic: $300,000-500,000
    • Key Assumptions:
  • 1-2% market share of ENS operations
  • $50k-75k monthly marketplace volume
  • 2-5 premium name sales per month
  • Steady growth in fixed fee operations
    • Primary Revenue Driver: Marketplace fees (especially premium name sales)

    Notes

  • All estimates assume ETH price of $2,500
  • Premium name sales have highest revenue potential per transaction
  • Fixed fees provide steady, predictable income
  • Marketplace fees scale with volume (primary growth driver)
  • Revenue estimates are pre-tax and pre-operating costs
