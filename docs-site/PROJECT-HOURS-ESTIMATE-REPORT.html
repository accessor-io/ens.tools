Report Date: January 2025 Project: ENS.Tools - Enterprise ENS Management Platform Total Estimated Hours: 450-550 hours Total Estimated Value: $33,750 - $82,500 (at $75-$150/hour)

Executive Summary

This report provides an itemized breakdown of development hours for the ENS.Tools project, with supporting evidence from the codebase. The project is a full-stack TypeScript/React application providing enterprise-grade ENS domain management, contract registry, DAO registry, marketplace integration, and ENSIP-19 compliance.

  • Total Lines of Code: ~36,000 TypeScript/TSX
  • Source Files: 64 TypeScript services + 82 React components
  • UI Components: 46 shadcn/ui components

Annual to Hourly Rate Conversion:

Full-Time Employee Rate Calculation:

  • Annual salary ÷ 2,080 hours (40 hours/week × 52 weeks) = hourly equivalent
    • 

  • Senior Web3 ($160k-$220k) = $77-$106/hour (employee rate)
    • 

Contractor/Freelance Rate Calculation:
Contractor rates typically 1.5-2x employee rates due to:

  • No benefits (health insurance, retirement, PTO)
    • 

  • Self-employment taxes
    • 

  • Business overhead
    • 

  • Project risk/uncertainty
    • 

  • Higher demand for specialized skills
    • 


  • Conservative (1.5x): $77-$106 × 1.5 = $116-$159/hour
    • 

  • Market (1.75x): $77-$106 × 1.75 = $135-$186/hour
    • 

  • Premium (2x): $77-$106 × 2 = $154-$212/hour
    • 

Market Alignment:
The $75-$150/hour range used in this report is conservative and aligns with:

  • Lower end (1x multiplier) for senior full-time rates
    • 

  • Upper end (1.5x multiplier) for contractor rates
    • 

  • Accounts for market variations and project complexity
